PRIVACY POLICY.

How CNE Zone collects, uses and protects your information.

Last updated: January 2026

CNE Zone ("we", "us", "our") operates the website cnezone.github.io (the "Site"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit or use this Site, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using this Site, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information in two ways: information you give us directly, and information collected automatically while you browse.

  • Information you provide: When you submit our contact form, this includes your name, company, email address, phone number, selected service and message content. This is sent to our team via a third-party form-processing service (FormSubmit) so we can respond to your inquiry.
  • Automatically collected information: Like most websites, our Site may use cookies, browser storage and similar technologies to remember basic preferences and to understand how visitors use the Site. If third-party advertising (such as Google AdSense) is enabled on this Site, Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites.

2. How We Use Information

  • To respond to inquiries submitted through our contact form
  • To schedule site surveys and provide service quotations
  • To improve our website content, layout and navigation
  • To comply with legal obligations where applicable

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information to third parties for their own marketing purposes.

3. Cookies & Advertising

This Site may display advertisements served by third-party advertising companies, including Google AdSense. These companies may use cookies, web beacons or similar technologies to collect information about your visits to this and other websites in order to provide advertisements about goods and services that may be of interest to you.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to our website or other websites. For more information on how Google uses data, visit Google's Partner Sites policy.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services, each of which has its own privacy policy governing how they handle data:

  • FormSubmit — processes our contact form submissions and forwards them to our email
  • Google Fonts & Font Awesome — deliver typography and icons used on this Site
  • WhatsApp — used for the WhatsApp contact button, subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy

5. Data Retention & Security

Inquiry information submitted via our contact form is retained only as long as necessary to respond to your request and maintain business records. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration or disclosure, though no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal information you have submitted to us by emailing cne.zonee@gmail.com. You may also disable cookies through your browser settings at any time, though some parts of the Site may not function as intended.

7. Children's Privacy

This Site is intended for business and professional use and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
