CNE Zone Tools

PLAN YOUR SITE BEFORE WE VISIT.

Practical tools built by our field team, free to use in your browser. Start with the CCTV layout planner and turn a floor plan into a marked-up map and equipment list.

Featured Tool

CCTV Layout & Site Survey Planner.

Upload a floor plan as a PDF, place cameras and access control devices exactly where you want them, link them with cable runs and export a clean map together with a bill of quantities (BOQ). It is the same method our engineers use during a site survey.

  • Mark bullet, dome and PTZ cameras, poles, switches, face and ANPR devices
  • Add access control readers and time and attendance terminals
  • Switch between CCTV and access control layers
  • Export the marked map and the BOQ as PDF files
  • Save your work as a project file and continue later

Launch The Planner

Your Files Stay With You

Runs Entirely In Your Browser.

The planner works on your own device. The floor plan you open is not uploaded to our website or to any server, and your saved project file stays wherever you store it. Close the tab and nothing is kept unless you save it yourself.

The tool loads a few open-source libraries (PDF reading, PDF export and styling) from public content delivery networks, so an internet connection is needed the first time it opens.

How It Works

From Floor Plan
To BOQ.

01

Open A Plan

Load a PDF drawing of your floor or site. The page becomes the background of the working area.

02

Place Devices

Choose a device type and click on the plan to place it. Move, select and delete devices as the design changes.

03

Link & Review

Connect devices with cable runs, check the live count of each device type and filter by layer.

04

Export

Download the marked map and the BOQ as PDFs, or save a project file to edit again later.

Who It Is For

Owners, Managers And Installers.

Building owners can sketch what they need before asking for quotes, which makes proposals easier to compare. Facility managers can document existing devices. Installers can prepare a survey drawing in minutes instead of marking paper plans by hand.

Tips For Best Results
  • Use a clear PDF plan with readable walls and doors
  • Cover entrances, corners and cash areas first
  • Keep a saved project file as your editable master copy
  • Treat the BOQ as a starting point; final quantities are confirmed after an on-site survey
Bandwidth & Storage

Official Manufacturer
Calculators.

Before you finalise camera settings, estimate the network bandwidth and recorder storage your design will need. These calculators are provided by the camera manufacturers themselves and open on their own websites.

Hikvision HiTools

Hikvision's design toolbox includes calculators for bandwidth and storage, useful when your project uses Hikvision cameras and recorders.

Open Hikvision tool

Dahua Disk & Bandwidth Calculator

Dahua's calculator estimates the disk capacity and bandwidth needed for a given number of cameras, bitrate and retention period.

Open Dahua calculator

These external tools belong to Hikvision and Dahua and are not operated by CNE Zone. Results are estimates; real bandwidth and storage depend on the scene, motion, lighting, compression and camera settings. We confirm final figures during a site survey.
